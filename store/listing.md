## Description (4000 max)

Scan Kit turns your iPhone or iPad into a fast, private document scanner. Scan paper, find any word, fill in and sign PDFs, and keep every file on your device. No account, no subscription, no ads.

SCAN IN SECONDS
• Point the camera at a page and Scan Kit finds the edges and takes the photo for you.
• Pages come out flat, sharp and easy to read, in colour, greyscale or black and white.
• Scan many pages in a row, then reorder, crop or rotate them.
• Special modes for receipts, books, whiteboards, ID cards and business cards.
• Import photos and PDFs you already have.

FIND ANY WORD
• Scan Kit reads the text on every page, right on your device.
• Search all your documents for a word, a name or an amount.
• Find text highlights every match on the page and steps through them one by one, with zoom.
• Copy text from any page, share it or have it read aloud.
• Tap a date on a page to add it to Calendar, or save a business card to Contacts.

FILL AND SIGN
• Fill in PDF forms: text fields, checkboxes, choices and dates.
• Draw your signature once and place it on any document.
• Add initials, ticks, crosses and dates anywhere on the page.

21 FREE TOOLS
• Merge, split and extract pages.
• Compress PDFs to a size you choose.
• Protect PDFs with a password, or unlock them.
• Redact private details, add a watermark or a timestamp.
• Erase marks: lift pencil, highlighter and shadows from a page, and compare before and after first.
• Flatten PDFs, make one long image, and work with QR codes.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Everything happens on your device. Your documents are never uploaded.
• No account, no sign-in, no tracking and no ads.
• Lock the app with Face ID or your passcode.
• Recently deleted keeps removed documents for 30 days, in case you change your mind.
• Export all your documents as searchable PDFs in one ZIP whenever you like.

Made for iPhone and iPad, in light and dark mode.
