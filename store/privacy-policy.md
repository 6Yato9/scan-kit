# Scan Kit privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Scan Kit is made by ZERO HORIZON LTD ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Scan Kit on iPhone, iPad or Android.

## The short version

Scan Kit does not collect any of your data. Your documents stay on your device.

## Your documents

The pages you scan, the PDFs and photos you import, and the text Scan Kit reads from them are stored only on your device, inside the app. They are not uploaded to us or to anyone else. Text recognition runs on your device.

A document leaves your device only when you choose to send it somewhere, for example with Share, Print or Export. Where it goes then is up to you and the app or service you pick.

## No account, no tracking

Scan Kit has no account or sign-in. It contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not collect device identifiers, location or usage data.

## Permissions

- Camera: to scan documents. Photos taken in Scan Kit stay in the app unless you export them.
- Photos: to import pictures you choose, and to save images when you ask.
- Face ID: to unlock the app when App Lock is on. Apple handles Face ID; we never see it.
- Calendar and Contacts: only when you tap a date or contact detail to add it. Scan Kit adds what you confirm and reads nothing else.

## Deleting your data

Deleted documents go to Recently deleted for 30 days, then they are removed. Deleting the app removes all of its documents from your device. Device backups (such as iCloud Backup) are controlled by your device settings.

## Children

Scan Kit is suitable for everyone and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
